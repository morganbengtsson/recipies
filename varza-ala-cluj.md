# Varza ala cluj

## Ingredients

* 1 kg minced meat
* Smoked sausage
* Wine
* 400g canned tomatoes
* Tomato paste
* 2 onions
* 2 tablespoons oil
* 1 teaspoon dried thyme
* 2 teaspoon sweet paprika

* 1.5kg sauerkraut. Somewhat rinced depending on soureness.
* 2 onions
* 2 tablespoons oil
* 1 teaspoon cumin seeds

* 180 g rice

* salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Boil rice. A bit al-dente.
2. Fry chopped onions in oil. Add sauerkraut and simmer for about 20min
3. Fry the other chopped onions in oil. Add minced meat and choped smoked sausage. Fry some more, add crushed tomatoes and simmer for a bit.
4. Assemble in two baking trays with a bit of oil in the bottom. Cabbage, rice, meat and cabbage again.
5. Cook in oven 190°C for ~30 minutes
